# MinCal

**Access G-Cal independently**

MinCal is an extremely minimal client for Google Calendar. Unlike the official app, MinCal lets you access your calendar without logging into other Google apps on your device.

[<img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/713d71c5-3dec-4ec4-a3f2-8d28d025a9c6" alt="Get it on Obtainium" height="80">](https://apps.obtainium.imranr.dev/redirect?r=obtainium://add/https://github.com/BURG3R5/MinCal)

## Features

- **Notifications** - Get notified of upcoming events 🔔
- **Private** - Use your own OAuth credentials 🔐
- **Independent** - Device-wide Google login is not required 🚫
- **Free & open-source**
  - No ads, subscriptions, or in-app purchases 🆓
  - Licensed under the [GPLv3](https://github.com/BURG3R5/MinCal/blob/dev/license) 📃
- **Lightweight** - Runs in the background with minimal battery and memory usage ⏩
